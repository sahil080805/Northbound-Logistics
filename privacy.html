Privacy policy

How Northbound Logistics collects, uses and protects your information.

Sample policy for a demonstration website. Have your legal team review and adapt it before using it for a real business.

What we collect

When you submit a quote or contact form we collect the details you provide: name, company, email, phone number and shipment information.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotes
  • To arrange and track shipments
  • To improve our services and support

Sharing

We don't sell your data. We share information only with carriers, partners and service providers who need it to fulfil your shipment, or where required by law.

Retention & security

We keep enquiry data only as long as necessary and use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@northboundlogistics.in.
