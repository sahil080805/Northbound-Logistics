Our story

Started with six trucks. Still obsessed with the details.

Northbound began in a small Noida yard in 2008. Our founders had seen how often shippers were left in the dark — no updates, no accountability. We set out to build the opposite.

Today we operate 1,400+ vehicles and 40+ warehouses, and we've delivered over 2.4 million shipments. The scale has changed; the principle hasn't.

Our mission

To make freight movement simple, visible and dependable for every business, from a first-time exporter to a national retailer.

Our vision

To be India's most trusted logistics partner — known for reliability, transparency and a lighter footprint on the planet.

What we stand for

Our values
