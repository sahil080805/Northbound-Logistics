FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How do I book a shipment?

Request a quote online or call our desk. Once you approve the quote we schedule pickup — usually within 24 hours for domestic road freight.

Can I track my cargo?

Yes. Every shipment gets a tracking ID (format NB1234567). Enter it on our tracking page or follow the SMS/email milestone alerts.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on service, weight or volume, distance, cargo type and timing. Quotes are itemised, so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

Is my cargo insured?

Basic carrier liability is included. For high-value or fragile consignments we recommend all-risk cargo insurance, which we can arrange at booking.

What can't you carry?

We don't carry illegal goods, live animals or items prohibited by law. Dangerous goods are accepted only with proper classification and documentation.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Our sea and air teams manage import/export documentation and clearance, so you deal with one provider instead of three.
